Approach

Where cultural ambition and brand vision meet.

ENCOUNTER’s advisory work spans six pillars, from assessment to impact, ensuring a high standard of execution while supporting the teams closely and giving them the tools to succeed. Engaged in full or at any stage, as the project requires.

01 / Mapping
Audit · Competitive Benchmark · Asset Valuation · Cultural DNA

Assess what makes a cultural offering distinctive, identify the gaps, and align the vision with the objectives.

02 / Strategy
Positioning · Brand Partner Intelligence · Platform Identification · Narrative Development

Identify the cultural territories of influence, and define the partners with the strongest relevance and long-term alignment.

03 / Concept
Ideation · Brand Activation · Exclusive Experiences · Cultural Programming · Community Building

Design bespoke creative formats that bring partnerships to life — from high-profile commissions to multi-year global programming.

04 / Connection
Global Network · High-Level Introductions · Tailored Pitch & Proposal · Partnership Architecture

Draw on an established global network to make the right introductions, with a tailor-made pitch and proposal carefully crafted for each partner.

05 / Negotiation
Contract Negotiation · Governance Frameworks · Commercial Terms · Stewardship · Relationship Building

Protect intellectual property and set clear governance so creative freedom and commercial protection coexist.

06 / Measurement
Impact Assessment · Return on Investment · Optimization · Reputational Reporting

Track impact across cultural, commercial and reputational return, and refine each program so its value grows over time.
